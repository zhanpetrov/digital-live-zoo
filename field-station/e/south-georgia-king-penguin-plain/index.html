What to look for

  • Glacial outwash colony plain — Aptenodytes patagonicus crèche zone. Centre the outwash plain where nestless king penguins space by flipper-reach
  • Elephant-seal & lanternfish surf — Antarctic Polar Front commute. Locate the beach surf zone launching 300 m dives toward the Polar Front
  • Allardyce Range barrier glaciers — Katabatic wind shelter. Scan the 2,900 m mountain spine sheltering northeast South Georgia

Source: Aubin & Jouventin (1998), Proc. R. Soc. B — 'Cocktail-party effect' acoustic recognition in king penguin colonies.

Field quiz

Kids' question: How does a returning king penguin find its own chick among thousands of noisy neighbors?

  • A unique two-note trumpet call that works like a secret voice password!
  • Reading numbers on flippers
  • Following footprints in wet sand
Answer

A unique two-note trumpet call that works like a secret voice password!

Adults' question: How long does it take a single king penguin pair to fledge one chick through a subantarctic winter?

  • 14 to 16 months—including overwinter chick fasting in downy crèches
  • Just 28 days in midsummer
  • Exactly 5 years underground
  • Two weeks before autumn freeze-up
Answer

14 to 16 months—including overwinter chick fasting in downy crèches
