About this place

Explore the Great American Biotic Interchange corridor, vertical rainforest stratification, and Ramphastos bill thermoregulation.

For kids: Climb into a leafy rainforest skyscraper on the narrow bridge where North and South American wildlife met!

Panama Canopy Tower & Land Bridge · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Emergent almendro toucan crown — Dipteryx oleifera & Ramphastos sulfuratus. Scan the emergent Dipteryx crown frequented by keel-billed toucans
  • Mid-canopy howler monkey highway — Alouatta palliata acoustic stratum. Centre the mid-story liana network linking Alouatta palliata leaf-browsing territories
  • Shaded motmot pendulum understory — Electron platyrhynchum pursuit-deterrent. Locate the dim sub-canopy perches where motmots perform wag-display signaling

Source: O'Dea et al. (2016), Science Advances & Tattersall et al. (2009), Science — Isthmus of Panama formation and Ramphastos thermal radiator bills.

Field quiz

Kids' question: Besides reaching berries on thin twigs, what secret job does a keel-billed toucan's giant colorful beak do?

  • It cools the bird down like a built-in heat radiator!
  • It stores gallons of rainwater inside
  • It glows like a flashlight inside hollow trees
Answer

It cools the bird down like a built-in heat radiator!

Adults' question: What biogeographic milestone occurred when tectonic uplift completed the Isthmus of Panama land bridge?

  • It launched the Great American Biotic Interchange ashore while isolating Caribbean and Pacific marine faunas
  • It connected South America directly to West Africa
  • It drained the Amazon Basin westward into the Pacific
  • It froze equatorial coral reefs under continental glaciers
Answer

It launched the Great American Biotic Interchange ashore while isolating Caribbean and Pacific marine faunas
