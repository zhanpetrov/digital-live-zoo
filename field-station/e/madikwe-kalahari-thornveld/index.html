About this place

Investigate Lycaon pictus acoustic sneeze-quorum decision-making (Walker et al., 2017) and hyper-cooperative pack care.

For kids: Meet patchy-coated African painted dogs who hold friendly pep rallies and vote on when to trot off by sneezing!

What to look for

  • Red-sand pack rally clearing — Lycaon pictus acoustic quorum arena. Centre the open sandveld arena where packs perform pre-departure social rallies
  • Kalahari umbrella-thorn shade — Vachellia tortilis ecotone savanna. Locate the Vachellia tortilis canopy marking the Kalahari-Lowveld biogeographic ecotone
  • Open bushveld trotting track — Cursorial endurance & pinna cooling. Scan the open cursorial corridor navigated via white tail flags and radar-dish pinnae

Source: Walker, King, McNutt & Jordan (2017), Proceedings of the Royal Society B — Sneeze to leave: African wild dogs (Lycaon pictus) use variable quorum thresholds.

Field quiz

Kids' question: How does a pack of African painted dogs vote on whether everyone is ready to head out from a rest stop?

  • By letting out sharp little sneezes until enough packmates agree!
  • By stacking colorful pebbles in a pile
  • By howling only when rainbows appear
Answer

By letting out sharp little sneezes until enough packmates agree!

Adults' question: In Walker et al. (2017), how did social rank modify the sneeze-quorum threshold in Lycaon pictus pre-departure rallies?

  • Dominant-initiated rallies required ~3 sneezes, whereas subordinate-initiated rallies needed ~10 sneezes
  • Only pups under three months old were permitted to vocalize
  • Sneezes cancelled all departures for twenty-four hours
  • Rank had zero effect because packs never rest together
Answer

Dominant-initiated rallies required ~3 sneezes, whereas subordinate-initiated rallies needed ~10 sneezes
