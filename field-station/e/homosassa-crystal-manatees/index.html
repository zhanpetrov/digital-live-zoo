About this place

Study Trichechus manatus low metabolic thermoregulation, karst spring refugia, and pachyosteosclerotic ribs.

For kids: Swim into crystal-clear warm springs where gentle sea cows wear built-in weight belts of solid bone!

Homosassa Warm-Spring Manatees · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Warm limestone aquifer vent — Floridan Aquifer 22 °C thermal refuge. Centre the karst limestone vent discharging constant 22 °C groundwater
  • Submerged eelgrass meadow — Pachyosteosclerotic benthic trim. Locate the submerged macrophyte bed grazed via solid-rib neutral buoyancy
  • Cleaning-fish & cypress halo — Euryhaline fish & epibiont symbiosis. Scan the sunlit midwater column shared by mangrove snappers, mullet, and needlefish

Source: Irvine (1983), Biological Conservation; Domning & de Buffrénil (1991), Marine Mammal Science — Manatee thermal limits & bone ballast.

Adults' question: Which anatomical adaptation allows Trichechus manatus to hover horizontally over seagrass beds without expending energy?

  • Solid pachyosteosclerotic ribs lacking marrow cavities paired with elongated dorsal lungs
  • Swallowing twenty kilograms of granite cobbles weekly
  • Continuous downward thrust from dorsal fin undulations
  • Thick subdermal blubber denser than limestone
Answer

Solid pachyosteosclerotic ribs lacking marrow cavities paired with elongated dorsal lungs
