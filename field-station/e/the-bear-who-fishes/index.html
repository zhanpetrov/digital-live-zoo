Generated 360° wildlife reconstruction (July · Peak Sockeye Salmon Run · Permitted Remote Camera). Camera role: a wet basalt boulder right on the lip of brooks falls, as salmon leap past. Five sustained stationary shots joined by 1 s fades, starting at yaw 0° with synchronized subtitles and spatialized natural sound.

What to look for

  • Dominant Bear on the Waterfall Lip — Ursus arctos × Oncorhynchus nerka bottleneck. Locate the prime basalt lip station where dominant Ursus arctos intercept leaping sockeye (yaw 0°)
  • Shallow Gravel Margin (Mother & Cubs Zone) — Maternal spatial segregation · Ben-David et al. (2004). Scan left (-65°) to the slackwater gravel margin used by maternal females with cubs
