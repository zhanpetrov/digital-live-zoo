About this place

Examine Bison bonasus genetic recovery from twelve founders and calcareous sedge-fen hydrology sustaining aquatic warblers.

For kids: Explore giant spongy sedge marshes where shaggy European bison trim grass for tiny striped songbirds!

Polesie Primeval Fen & Bison · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Calcareous sedge tussocks — Acrocephalus paludicola microhabitat. Locate the chalk-rich sedge tussock zone used by nesting aquatic warblers
  • Alder-pine wisent browse line — Bison bonasus ecotone grazing. Centre the wet alder-birch forest margin browsed by European wisents
  • Shallow crane roosting pool — Grus grus acoustic safety roost. Scan the open fen water sheet serving as an anti-predator night roost for cranes

Source: Tokarska et al. (2011), Molecular Ecology & Tanneberger et al. (2010) — Bison bonasus twelve-founder bottleneck and calcareous fen management for Acrocephalus paludicola.

Adults' question: From how many captive founder individuals was the entire modern lowland-caucasian population of Bison bonasus restored?

  • Just twelve founders surviving in zoos after wild extirpation
  • Over five thousand wild bulls from Alpine glaciers
  • A single cloned mammoth cell line
  • Four hundred domestic cattle hybrids imported from Spain
Answer

Just twelve founders surviving in zoos after wild extirpation

By day
Eurasian cranes, moose wading in sedges, harriers & aquatic warblers Bubnów forms part of Europe's largest calcareous fen complex, hosting dense breeding territories of globally threatened aquatic warblers.
By night
Booming bitterns, chorusing tree frogs & thousands of roosting cranes Autumn evenings draw thousands of staging Grus grus into shallow fen pools, creating rich nocturnal biophony with Hyla orientalis.
