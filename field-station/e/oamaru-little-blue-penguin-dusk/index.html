About this place

Investigate Eudyptula minor synchronous dusk 'raft' anti-predator behavior, structural blue barbule optics, and pelagic countershading.

For kids: Meet the world's smallest penguins—bowling-pin-tall swimmers in slate-blue coats that team up offshore and march home together at dusk!

Oamaru Little Blue Penguin Rafts · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Offshore twilight 'raft' staging bay — Selfish-herd & nocturnal arrival sync. Centre the nearshore staging zone where foraging penguins coalesce before dusk
  • Sunlit turquoise surf & kelp rim — β-keratin nanofiber structural blue. Locate the coastal surf zone matching structural blue dorsal countershading
  • Basalt boulder & burrow slope — Crevice & burrow microclimate nesting. Scan the coastal boulder talus sheltering insulated nesting burrows

Source: Daniel et al. (2007), Animal Behaviour — Synchronous dusk raft arrivals in Eudyptula minor; Shawkey et al. (2011), Biology Letters — Keratin nanofiber blue plumage.

Ask together: Why is waiting until twilight AND walking up the beach in one big group of fifty friends so much safer for tiny penguins than coming home one by one at noon?

Field quiz

Kids' question: What is a floating group of little blue penguins waiting together offshore for sunset called?

  • A 'raft'—teaming up in one big group to cross the beach safely at twilight!
  • A submarine parade that stays underwater for weeks
  • An iceberg sled team
Answer

A 'raft'—teaming up in one big group to cross the beach safely at twilight!

Adults' question: What creates the distinctive indigo-slate color on the dorsal plumage of Eudyptula minor (Shawkey et al., 2011)?

  • Parallel arrays of β-keratin nanofibers inside feather barbules coherently scattering blue light
  • Blueberry pigments absorbed from coastal tundra shrubs
  • Bioluminescent bacteria coating waterproof preen oil
  • Copper-sulfate crystals precipitated from sea spray

Parallel arrays of β-keratin nanofibers inside feather barbules coherently scattering blue light
