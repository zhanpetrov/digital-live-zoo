What to look for

  • Bison dust-bath wallow bowl — Compacted vernal pool depression. Locate a compacted bison wallow depression that retains snowmelt
  • Patchwork prairie grazing lawn — Rotational graminoid-forb mosaic. Centre the grazed short-turf patch favouring forbs and burrowing owls
  • Wind-swept coulee ridge — Cranial snow-plowing ridge. Scan the exposed ridge where massive shoulder humps power winter snow-sweeping

Source: Knapp et al. (1999), BioScience — The keystone role of bison in North American tallgrass and mixed-grass prairie.

Kids' question: Why are red-orange baby bison nicknamed 'red dogs' on the sunny spring prairie?

  • They are born with bright cinnamon coats before growing dark brown humps
  • They bark like puppies at clouds
  • They paint themselves with red berries
Answer

They are born with bright cinnamon coats before growing dark brown humps

Adults' question: How do plains bison create breeding habitat for both short-grass plovers and tall-grass sparrows simultaneously?

  • Patchy rotational grazing and wallowing generate a heterogeneous structural mosaic across the landscape
  • Bison plant imported forest shrubs in straight rows
  • Continuous uniform mowing reduces the entire plain to bare dirt
  • Bison weave elevated grass nests on their horns
Answer

Patchy rotational grazing and wallowing generate a heterogeneous structural mosaic across the landscape
