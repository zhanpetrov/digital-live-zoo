About this place

Study Cervus canadensis vascularized velvet-antler osteogenesis and dual-register autumn bugle bioacoustics.

For kids: Listen to musical autumn bugle calls and marvel at antlers that grow as fast as a ruler edge every two weeks!

Appalachian Velvet-Antler Elk · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Misty clover bugling meadow — Biphonic vocalization arena. Centre the valley meadow amplifying biphonic autumn bugle displays
  • Oak & maple antler-polishing edge — Neural-crest velvet osteogenesis. Locate hardwood saplings rubbed as calcified antlers shed summer velvet
  • Restored Allegheny oak ridge — Quercus mast & mine-land reclamation. Scan the oak-hickory ridge restored from historic coal lands into prime elk range

Source: Li et al. (2014), Anatomical Record; Reby et al. (2016), Journal of Experimental Biology — Antler stem-cell osteogenesis & biphonic elk bugles.
