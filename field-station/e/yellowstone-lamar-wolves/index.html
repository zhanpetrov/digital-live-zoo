About this place

Yellowstone Lamar Wolves · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Rebounding streamside willows — Salix & Populus browse release. Locate the riparian willow corridor released from stationary elk browsing
  • Beaver dam wetland pocket — Castor canadensis ecosystem engineering. Centre the floodplain channel where beaver dams store groundwater
  • Sagebrush rendezvous ridge — Canis lupus acoustic & scent vantage. Scan the elevated glacial moraine used by wolf packs to survey ungulate herds

Source: Estes et al. (2011), Science; Ripple & Beschta (2012), Biological Conservation — Apex wolf trophic cascades in Yellowstone.
