About this place

Examine Sequoia sempervirens foliar fog uptake, canopy humus mats, and arboreal Aneides vagrans salamanders.

For kids: Craning your neck up Earth's tallest trees, discover secret rooftop fern gardens where sky-diving salamanders live!

Coast Redwood Sky Gardens · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Sky-island fern soil mat — Polypodium scouleri & Aneides vagrans. Scan the upper crown crotches supporting suspended fern soil mats
  • Spongy fire-shield redwood bark — Tannin-rich bark & basal lignotuber burls. Centre the fluted trunk armoured in foot-thick, resin-free tannin bark
  • Fog-drip redwood sorrel carpet — Oxalis oregana solar-tracking understory. Locate the shaded Oxalis understory hydrated by summer crown fog-drip

Source: Burgess & Dawson (2004), Plant, Cell & Environment; Spickler et al. (2006) — Redwood foliar fog uptake & canopy salamanders.

Adults' question: Why is foliar fog absorption vital for emergent Sequoia sempervirens shoots above 100 metres?

  • Reverse sap flow through leaf cuticles bypasses gravitational xylem tension limits near 122–130 metres
  • Salt crystals inside fog melt winter snow
  • Redwoods shed all needles every July
  • Roots drill straight into molten magma
