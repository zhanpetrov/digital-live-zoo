Field quiz

Kids' question: How does a sand tiger shark float weightlessly in place without sinking, even though sharks have no swim bladder?

  • It swims up to gulp a bubble of surface air and holds it inside its stomach
  • It ties buoyant seaweeds around its tail
  • Its skeleton is made of hollow dry cork
Answer

It swims up to gulp a bubble of surface air and holds it inside its stomach

Adults' question: What makes Carcharias taurus unique among all known sharks in its mechanism of hydrostatic buoyancy control?

  • Calcifying hollow gas chambers inside the dorsal fin spines
  • Continuous supersonic tail oscillation at 60 Hz

Gulping atmospheric air at the surface and retaining a regulated gas pocket in the stomach
