About this place

Explore Indonesian Throughflow larval connectivity, three-quarters of global scleractinian diversity, and walking-shark hypoxia tolerance.

For kids: Dive into Earth's richest underwater city—where giant manta rays visit fish car-washes and little reef sharks stroll on their fins!

Raja Ampat Coral Triangle Reefs · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Current-swept manta cleaning bommie — Mobula alfredi — Labroides mutualism. Centre the current-facing coral pinnacle hosting Labroides cleaner guilds
  • Indonesian Throughflow coral garden — Pacific–Indian Ocean larval highway. Locate the high-flow scleractinian and octocoral slope fed by upwelling
  • Tidal reef flat of walking sharks — Hemiscyllium halmahera & freycineti. Scan the intertidal rubble flat patrolled by endemic Hemiscyllium sharks

Source: Veron et al. (2009), Galaxea — Delineating the Coral Triangle & Renshaw et al. (2002) / Vanderwright et al. (2020) — Hemiscyllium walking-shark hypoxia physiology.
