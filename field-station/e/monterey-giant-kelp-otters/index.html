About this place

Examine Enhydra lutris pelage insulation, rock-anvil tool use, and urchin control protecting Macrocystis forests.

For kids: Float with tool-using sea otters wearing Earth's fluffiest fur coat to guard underwater kelp forests!

Monterey Kelp & Sea Otters · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Floating kelp seat-belt raft — Macrocystis pyrifera surface canopy. Centre the gas-bladder kelp canopy anchoring resting sea otter rafts
  • Reef foraging & pocket-rock zone — Strongylocentrotus purpuratus control. Locate the subtidal reef shelf where otters forage with chest-pouch stones
  • Upwelling coastal granite point — California Current nutrient upwelling. Scan the wind-driven coastal headland channeling cold nutrient-rich water

Source: Estes & Palmisano (1974), Science; Tinker et al. (2008), PNAS — Sea otter keystone predation & stone-tool specialization.

Field quiz

Kids' question: How does a sea otter keep its favorite shell-cracking rock safe while diving?

  • Tucked inside a cozy skin pocket under its arm
  • Balanced on the tip of its nose
  • Tied to its tail with seaweed
Answer

Tucked inside a cozy skin pocket under its arm

Adults' question: What happens to coastal Macrocystis forests when keystone sea otters are absent and purple urchins proliferate?

  • Urchins sever kelp holdfasts, shifting carbon-rich forests into bare urchin barrens
  • Kelp fronds calcify into hard tropical coral
  • Purple urchins plant new kelp spores deeper offshore
  • Water temperature boils away coastal nutrients
Answer

Urchins sever kelp holdfasts, shifting carbon-rich forests into bare urchin barrens
