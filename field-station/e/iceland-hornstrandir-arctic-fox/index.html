About this place

Examine Vulpes lagopus coastal 'blue' colour-morph genetics, countercurrent paw heat exchange, and seabird cliff ecology.

For kids: Explore towering green sea cliffs where Iceland's native blue-brown Arctic foxes tiptoe below puffin burrows!

Iceland Arctic Fox Sea Cliffs · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Turf-capped puffin cliff ledge — Fratercula arctica burrow terrace. Locate the vegetated basalt terrace honeycombed with alcid burrows
  • Dark basalt intertidal boulders — Coastal 'blue' Vulpes lagopus foraging zone. Centre the ice-free intertidal boulder zone matching the coastal blue fox morph
  • Denmark Strait gale horizon — Countercurrent rete mirabile paws. Survey the Denmark Strait marine horizon driving subarctic storms

Source: Våge et al. (2005), Genetics & Hersteinsson & Macdonald (1996) — MC1R mutation & adaptive habitat matching in coastal vs. tundra Arctic foxes.
