About this place

Study Spheniscus demersus supraorbital vasodilation thermoregulation and pelagic countershading on the Cape coast.

For kids: Meet warm-weather penguins in sunny South Africa who cool down using rosy pink windows right above their eyes!

Stony Point Tuxedo Penguins · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Sunlit granite penguin boulders — Supraorbital thermal window & spot ID. Centre the granite haul-out boulders where penguins regulate heat load
  • Cold kelp-forest surf zone — Ecklonia maxima & Thayer countershading. Locate the cold inshore kelp surf where penguins commute to pelagic baitballs
  • Dassie & cormorant rock ledges — Shared coastal seabird & Procavia colony. Scan the upper coastal ledges shared with endangered Crowned and Bank cormorants

Source: Frost, Siegfried & Greenwood (1976), Journal of Zoology — Behavioral & vascular thermoregulation in the African penguin (Spheniscus demersus).

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why do the bare patches above an African penguin's eyes turn bright rosy pink on a sunny day?

  • Warm blood flows there to let extra heat escape into the sea breeze
  • Because they eat pink cotton candy
  • To signal airplanes flying overhead
Answer

Warm blood flows there to let extra heat escape into the sea breeze

Adults' question: How does Spheniscus demersus prevent hyperthermia while incubating eggs ashore despite dense cold-water insulation?

  • Vasodilation in unfeathered supraorbital glands coupled with shaded burrow nesting
  • Shedding all waterproof contour feathers every afternoon
  • Sweating salt water from giant glands on their webbed feet
  • Panting underwater while holding their breath
Answer

Vasodilation in unfeathered supraorbital glands coupled with shaded burrow nesting
