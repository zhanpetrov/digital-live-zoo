About this place

Explore Gondwanan angiosperm refugia, Casuarius casuarius keystone megafaunal frugivory, casque thermoregulation, and infrasonic booms.

For kids: Step into Earth's oldest rainforest—where dinosaur-like cassowary dads plant giant plum trees and rumble deeper than piano keys!

Daintree Ancient Forest & Cassowaries · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Circular-frond Licuala fan-palm gallery — 180 Ma Gondwanan angiosperm refugium. Centre the Licuala ramsayi understory amidst basal Gondwanan angiosperms
  • Fallen blue-quandong & plum trail — Casuarius casuarius obligate endozoochory. Locate the forest-floor frugivory trail where >70 large-seeded species depend on cassowaries
  • Mossy rainforest creek & boom corridor — 23 Hz acoustic transmission & casque radiator. Scan the dense creek corridor carrying 23 Hz booms and cooling humid air

Source: Mack et al. (2003), Auk — Low-frequency ~23 Hz cassowary booms; Eastick et al. (2019), Scientific Reports — Casque thermal window; Bradford & Westcott (2010) — Keystone seed dispersal.

  • A controllable vascular 'thermal window' dumping excess metabolic heat in hot rainforest air and conserving heat in cool weather
