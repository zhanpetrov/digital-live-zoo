About this place

Examine the European montane-alpine treeline ecotone, cushion-plant thermal microclimates, and nocturnal ungulate guild partitioning.

For kids: Climb to sunny mountain meadows where round flower cushions trap their own cosy heat and nimble hoofed climbers share secret glades!

Alpine Timberline & Montane Ungulates · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Sun-warmed rock & cushion-plant crag — Silene acaulis boundary-layer dome & cloven-hoof grip. Locate the south-facing rock outcrop colonized by heat-trapping alpine cushion plants
  • Wind-flagged krummholz treeline — Growing-season ~6.4 °C isotherm treeline. Centre the stunted krummholz conifer ecotone governed by root-zone soil temperature
  • Montane mineral lick & stag glade — Cervus elaphus & Ovis gmelini mineral lick. Scan the volcanic mineral-seep glade visited nocturnally by red deer, mouflons, and wild boar

Source: Körner (2021), Alpine Plant Life (Springer) & Signer et al. (2011), J. Comp. Physiol. B — Alpine treeline isotherm physics, cushion-plant heat trapping, and ungulate seasonal heterothermy.

Field quiz

Kids' question: How do low, round cushion plants survive chilly winds high up on European mountains?

  • Packed stems block the wind and trap sunbeams like a warm mini-greenhouse
  • They pull up their roots and walk downhill every evening
  • Mountain goats knit tiny woollen hats for each flower
Answer

Packed stems block the wind and trap sunbeams like a warm mini-greenhouse

Adults' question: According to Körner's global synthesis, what primary physiological constraint sets the upper elevation limit of upright trees at the alpine timberline?

  • Thermal limitation of meristematic cell division and xylogenesis below ~6.4 °C growing-season mean rather than lack of photosynthesis
  • Complete absence of carbon dioxide molecules above two thousand metres
  • Excessive soil overheating boiling fine root hairs in midsummer
  • Total inability of conifer needles to survive winter frost
