About this place

Investigate thermotolerant urban Symbiodiniaceae coral holobionts and scarid parrotfish bioerosion.

For kids: Dive along sunny seawalls where super-tough brain corals team up with rainbow parrotfish toothbrushes!

Miami Coral City Resilient Reef · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Heat-hardy brain coral dome — Pseudodiploria & Durusdinium holobiont. Centre the grooved boulder coral hosting heat-tolerant Durusdinium symbionts
  • Parrotfish sand-maker reef — Sparisoma & Scarus pharyngeal mill. Locate the grazed carbonate pavement cleared for coral larval settlement
  • Gulf Stream tidal flush channel — Florida Current semi-diurnal exchange. Scan the tidal channel flushing urban corals with oxygenated Gulf Stream water

Source: Rohwer et al. (2002), Coral Reefs; Rubin et al. (2021), Communications Biology — Coral holobionts & heat-tolerant urban Miami genotypes.

By day

By night
Coral polyps extending feeding tentacles, octopuses, & spiny lobsters At night, stony corals switch from autotrophic algal sugars to heterotrophic zooplankton capture, while Caribbean reef octopuses emerge.
