What to look for

  • Grounded skyscraper icebergs — Archimedes 90% submerged draft. Centre the grounded tabular and pinnacle icebergs at the fjord moraine sill
  • Nutrient-rich meltwater channel — Calanus hyperboreus feeding lane. Locate the open-water channel where bowhead whales skim lipid-rich copepods
  • Greenland ice-sheet glow — Ice-blink atmospheric reflection. Scan the bright 'ice-blink' sky glare reflected off the inland ice sheet

Source: Keane et al. (2015), Cell Reports — Bowhead whale (Balaena mysticetus) genome reveals ERCC1/PCNA DNA-repair & >200-yr longevity.
