About this place

Analyze Megaptera novaeangliae spiral bubble-net acoustics and Savoca et al.'s Southern Ocean iron 'Whale Pump'.

For kids: Discover how giant humpback whales blow spiral bubble nets AND fertilize the ocean like giant sea gardeners!

Lemaire Channel Whale Pump · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Bubble-net & Whale-Pump channel — Euphausia superba & Fe recycling. Centre the calm fjord channel where spiral bubble nets trap krill
  • Twin-peak 'Kodak Gap' walls — Wind-shielded fjord stratification. Scan the 1,000 m glaciated cliffs sheltering Lemaire Channel from gales
  • Crabeater-seal ice floes — Lobodon carcinophaga krill sieve. Locate the drift-ice floes used by krill-filtering crabeater seals

Source: Roman & McCarthy (2010), PLoS ONE & Savoca et al. (2021), Nature — Baleen whale iron recycling & the krill paradox.

Ask together: Isn't it surprising that having MORE hungry whales in the ocean actually helps grow MORE krill and fish for everyone?

Field quiz

Kids' question: How do giant whales act like 'ocean gardeners' that help tiny sea plants and krill grow?

  • They bring rich nutrients up to sunny surface water so ocean plants bloom!
  • They plant seaweed seeds with their tails
  • They water coral reefs with buckets
Answer

They bring rich nutrients up to sunny surface water so ocean plants bloom!

Adults' question: What resolved the 20th-century 'Krill Paradox'—why Southern Ocean krill stocks declined rather than boomed after industrial whaling?

  • Whale fecal plumes recycled >10,000 t/yr of limiting iron into the euphotic zone, fueling diatom blooms that fed krill
  • Whales previously ate all krill predators exclusively
  • Ships accidentally removed sea salt
  • Krill require whale songs to hatch
Answer

Whale fecal plumes recycled >10,000 t/yr of limiting iron into the euphotic zone, fueling diatom blooms that fed krill
