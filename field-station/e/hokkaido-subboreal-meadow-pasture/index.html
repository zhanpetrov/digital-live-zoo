Investigate Hokkaido subboreal coastal grasslands, geothermal/spring-fed ice-free rivers, and Grus japonensis duet displays.

What to look for

  • Spring-fed steaming meadow stream — Groundwater thermal refugium. Locate the groundwater-fed stream corridor remaining ice-free in winter
  • Cool-season sward & grazing herd — Hindgut-fermentation thermogenesis. Centre the cool-season grass sward fueling equine cecal thermogenesis
  • Hidaka mountain breeze & crane skyway — Antiphonal unison-call acoustic corridor. Scan the coastal-mountain horizon carrying antiphonal crane duets
