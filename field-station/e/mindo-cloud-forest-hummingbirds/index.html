About this place

Investigate figure-eight leading-edge vortex aerodynamics, structural feather iridescence, and deep nocturnal torpor in Chocó-Andean hummingbirds.

For kids: Zoom into a misty orchid forest where jewel-bright hummingbirds draw sideways figure-eights in the air and fly backward!

What to look for

  • Heliconia & fuchsia nectar blossoms — Figure-eight leading-edge vortex zone. Centre the ornithophilous floral cluster visited by hovering trochilids
  • Mossy nocturnal torpor perch — Nocturnal heterothermic roost. Locate the sheltered epiphyte bough used for nocturnal metabolic torpor
  • Tank-bromeliad cloud canopy — Phytotelmata micro-ecosystems. Scan the upper cloud-forest crown loaded with water-impounding bromeliads

Source: Warrick, Tobalske & Powers (2005), Nature — Asymmetrical leading-edge vortices during figure-eight hummingbird hovering.

Ask together: Try tracing a sideways figure-eight in the air with both hands—how is that different from flapping straight up and down like a pigeon?

  • Tracing a sideways figure-eight that makes lift forward AND backward

Tracing a sideways figure-eight that makes lift forward AND backward
