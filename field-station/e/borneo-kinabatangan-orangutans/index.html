About this place

Study Pongo pygmaeus mast-fruiting spatial memory, herbal self-medication, cultural tool use, and eight-year maternal apprenticeship.

For kids: Swing high with clever red-haired orangutans that weave fresh leafy beds every evening and fashion rain umbrellas from giant leaves!

Borneo River-Canopy Orangutans · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Woven treetop night-nest fork — Composite structural arboreal platform. Centre the upper-canopy fork suited for green-wood fracture-weave nests
  • Fruiting riverine strangler fig — Ficus keystone fallback resource. Locate the riverine Ficus crown buffering inter-mast fruit scarcity
  • Medicinal lianas & floodplain bank — Zoopharmacognosy & arboreal sway. Scan the liana-draped riverbank used for tree-sway crossing and herbal salves

Source: van Schaik et al. (2003), Science — Orangutan cultural repertoires; Laumer et al. (2024), Scientific Reports — Active wound treatment with medicinal liana.

Ask together: If you had to weave your own bed high in a tree every single night before dark, how would you test if the branches were strong AND bouncy?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why does a young orangutan stay close to its mother for about eight whole years—longer than any other land animal besides humans?

  • To memorize hundreds of jungle foods, safe climbing paths, and leaf-tool tricks
  • Because young orangutans don't grow arms until age eight
  • To wait for snow to melt on Borneo
Answer

To memorize hundreds of jungle foods, safe climbing paths, and leaf-tool tricks

Adults' question: How do Bornean orangutans physiologically endure multi-year intervals of fruit scarcity between dipterocarp mast events?

  • Ultra-low primate daily energy throughput, lipid storage during masts, and bark/cambium fallback
  • Six-month dry-season estivation buried in river mud
  • Switching to active pack-hunting of pygmy elephants
  • Symbiotic nitrogen-fixing gills along the throat pouch
Answer

Ultra-low primate daily energy throughput, lipid storage during masts, and bark/cambium fallback
