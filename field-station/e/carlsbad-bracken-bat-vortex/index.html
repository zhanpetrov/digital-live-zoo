About this place

Examine Tadarida brasiliensis helical emergence vortices, sonar jamming avoidance, and high-altitude moth predation.

For kids: Watch a swirling sunset tornado of desert bats tune their voice pitches so nobody bumps in the dark!

Carlsbad Dusk Bat Vortex · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Limestone cave vortex mouth — Counterclockwise helical emergence. Centre the karst sinkhole opening where dense emergence columns form
  • Chihuahuan lechuguilla & agave slope — Agave lechuguilla xeric scrub. Locate the limestone desert scrub sheltering nocturnal insects and ringtails
  • High-altitude moth skyway — Tail-wind supersonic-record horizontal flight. Scan the upper boundary layer where free-tailed bats ride winds above 1,000 m

Source: Gillam et al. (2007), Behavioral Ecology; Kunz et al. (2011) — Tadarida brasiliensis jamming avoidance & agroecological pest control.
