About this place

Investigate Heteroconger mucus-cemented sand burrows, elasmobranch ampullae of Lorenzini electroreception, and Carcharhinus reef-edge patrols.

For kids: Peek into a bright white coral-sand highway where hundreds of garden eels pop up like periscopes and stingrays sense tiny electric sparks!

Mesoamerican Atoll Sand Channel & Sharks · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Swaying garden eel periscope field — Heteroconger longissimus mucus tube. Centre the current-swept carbonate sand flat colonized by Heteroconger longissimus
  • Stingray electric-sensor sand ripples — Hypanus americanus ampullae of Lorenzini. Locate the sand-channel floor excavated by electroreceptive Hypanus americanus
  • Cobalt atoll rim & reef shark pass — Carcharhinus perezi apex corridor. Scan the shelf-edge chute opening patrolled by Caribbean reef sharks

Source: Kalmijn (1971), J. Exp. Biol. & Bedore & Kajiura (2013) — Elasmobranch nanovolt electroreception via ampullae of Lorenzini & Heteroconger ecology.

Ask together: If you could sense tiny invisible electric sparks from every beating heart nearby, how easy would hide-and-seek be in the dark?

Field quiz

Kids' question: How does a southern stingray find a clam or crab buried completely out of sight underneath clean coral sand?

  • Jelly-filled pores under its nose sense tiny electric sparks from buried animals!
  • It knocks on every grain of sand with a shell
  • Garden eels point their noses at buried crabs
Answer

Jelly-filled pores under its nose sense tiny electric sparks from buried animals!

Adults' question: How did Kalmijn's classic aquarium experiments prove elasmobranchs hunt via pure bioelectric fields rather than scent or touch alone?

  • Sharks and rays struck accurately at agar chambers passing odour yet blocking vision, and vanished only when an insulating film blocked microvolt fields—then struck bare electrodes!
  • Rays relied exclusively on ultrasonic melon clicks
  • Buried flatfish glowed in infrared wavelengths through sand
  • Sharks magnetized iron sand grains with their teeth
Answer
