About this place

Study Phoenicopterus roseus lamellar filter-feeding, carotenoid pigmentation, and agro-ecological rice-lagoon hydrology.

For kids: Wade into sunny Spanish lagoons where pink flamingos eat upside-down with built-in strainer beaks!

Ebro Delta Flamingo Lagoons · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Brine-shrimp salt pan — Phoenicopterus roseus filter-feeding ring. Locate the hypersaline shallow pan where flamingos trample circular feeding troughs
  • Phragmites heron & ibis island — Plegadis falcinellus mixed colony. Centre the emergent Phragmites island sheltering colonial herons and glossy ibises
  • Organic rice-paddy flyway mosaic — Western Mediterranean Flyway stopover. Scan the flooded organic paddy-lagoon interface buffering deltaic salinity

Source: Amat et al. (2005), Behavioral Ecology and Sociobiology & Jenkin (1957) — Phoenicopterus roseus lamellar filter mechanics and cosmetic carotenoid preen-oil signaling.

Ask together: Flamingo chicks hatch snowy grey-white! What would happen to a flamingo's pink feathers if it switched to eating plain white rice?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Where do greater flamingos get their bright rosy-pink feather colour?

  • Natural red-orange pigments inside the tiny brine shrimp and algae they eat
  • Sunburn from standing in Spanish sunshine
  • Rolling in crushed strawberry petals
Answer

Natural red-orange pigments inside the tiny brine shrimp and algae they eat

Adults' question: How did Amat et al. (2005) show that greater flamingos actively enhance plumage colour prior to pair formation?

  • Rubbing carotenoid-enriched uropygial preen oil onto neck and breast feathers as avian 'cosmetics'
  • Secreting copper porphyrin pigments straight from sweat glands
  • Shedding outer barbules to expose pink hollow feather shafts
  • Basking in iron-oxide mud springs every full moon
Answer

Rubbing carotenoid-enriched uropygial preen oil onto neck and breast feathers as avian 'cosmetics'
