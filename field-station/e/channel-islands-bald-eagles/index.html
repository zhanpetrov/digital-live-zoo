What to look for

  • Sea-cliff stick eyrie — Haliaeetus leucocephalus coastal eyrie. Locate the windswept volcanic bluff supporting multi-year eagle eyries
  • Sparkling fish-hunting surf line — Plantar spicules & dual-fovea strike zone. Centre the nearshore upwelling channel patrolled via telephoto foveal vision
  • Island fox coastal sage hills — Urocyon littoralis trophic balance. Scan the coastal sage scrub where fish-eating bald eagles protect endemic island foxes

Source: Grier (1982), Science; Newsome et al. (2010), PNAS — Post-DDT Haliaeetus leucocephalus recovery & Channel Islands trophic ecology.

Adults' question: How did restoring marine-foraging bald eagles indirectly rescue the endemic Channel Island fox?

  • Territorial bald eagles exclude colonizing golden eagles that previously preyed on island foxes
  • Bald eagles drop fresh salmon inside fox dens
  • Eagles carry foxes between islands during storms
  • Eagle feathers fertilize coastal oak acorns
Answer

Territorial bald eagles exclude colonizing golden eagles that previously preyed on island foxes
