What to look for

  • Low-tide seal sandbar — Phoca vitulina thermoregulatory posture. Locate the emergent intertidal sandbar used for haul-out thermoregulation
  • Turbid flounder tidal channel — Vibrissal hydrodynamic wake tracking. Centre the turbid tidal gully where seals track benthic flatfish wakes
  • Diatom mudflat shorebird table — East Atlantic Flyway benthic refuelling. Scan the exposed intertidal mudflat supporting East Atlantic Flyway shorebirds

Source: Reijnders et al. (2010) & Dehnhardt et al. (2001), Science — Wadden Sea Phoca vitulina haul-out energetics and hydrodynamic whisker trail-following.
