About this place

What to look for

  • Glacier-polished granite skerry — Roche moutonnée isostatic coast. Locate the striated roche moutonnée granite headland rebounding after deglaciation
  • Clear sea-trout sound channel — Snell's-law plunge-dive vector. Centre the clear skerry channel where ospreys minimize refractive parallax
  • Wind-sculpted island crag nest — Insular predator-free eyrie. Scan the wind-sheared insular pine crag isolated from terrestrial mammalian nest predators

Source: Katzir & Intrator (1987) & Häkkinen (1978), Annales Zoologici Fennici — Avian refraction-angle compensation across air-water interfaces and Fennoscandian skerry osprey ecology.

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why does a swimming fish underwater actually sit deeper than it looks from up in the air?

  • Light rays bend as they leave the water into the air, just like a bent straw in a cup
  • Fish wear invisible stilts on the sea floor
  • Ocean waves magnify everything ten times bigger
Answer

Light rays bend as they leave the water into the air, just like a bent straw in a cup

Adults' question: How do plunge-diving ospreys reduce target-position error caused by Snell's-law refraction at the water surface?

  • Hovering at steep near-vertical angles where incident rays approach the surface normal and updating trajectory mid-plunge
  • Closing both eyes and relying strictly on ultrasonic echoes
  • Waiting until midnight when light refraction stops working
  • Dropping pebbles first to measure splash depth

Hovering at steep near-vertical angles where incident rays approach the surface normal and updating trajectory mid-plunge
