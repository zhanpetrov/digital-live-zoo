About this place

Investigate flooded caldera geothermal beaches and Libourel et al.'s (2023, Science) 4-second microsleep discovery in Pygoscelis antarcticus.

For kids: Walk a steaming black volcano beach in Antarctica where chinstrap penguins take ten thousand four-second naps a day!

Deception Volcanic Penguin Beach · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Steaming geothermal black beach — Submarine caldera fumarole sand. Centre the geothermal black-ash shoreline along the flooded caldera
  • Chinstrap colony caldera slopes — Pygoscelis antarcticus microsleep nests. Scan the ash-free caldera ridges supporting massive chinstrap colonies
  • Neptune's Bellows caldera gate — Collapsed caldera sea breach. Locate the breached caldera channel connecting Port Foster to Bransfield Strait

Source: Libourel et al. (2023), Science — Nesting chinstrap penguins accrue >11 hours of daily sleep via >10,000 four-second microsleeps.

Ask together: Count 'one-two-three-four' out loud—can you imagine waking up and falling back asleep that fast all day long to guard your egg?

Adults' question: What surprising conclusion did EEG loggers reveal about 4-second microsleeps in nesting chinstrap penguins (Libourel et al., 2023)?

  • Fragmented 4-second bouts genuinely fulfill restorative sleep functions across multi-week incubation shifts
  • Penguins never enter slow-wave brain activity ashore
  • Only chicks sleep while adults stay 100% awake for a month
  • Volcanic gases cause temporary fainting
Answer

Fragmented 4-second bouts genuinely fulfill restorative sleep functions across multi-week incubation shifts
