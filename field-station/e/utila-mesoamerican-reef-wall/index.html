About this place

Examine Mesoamerican Barrier Reef coral holobionts, Acanthurus herbivore grazing guilds, and Elacatinus cleaning symbioses.

For kids: Float along a sunlit Caribbean coral wall where blue tang schools mow seaweed lawns and tiny gobies run fish car-washes!

Utila Mesoamerican Reef Wall · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Boulder star coral holobiont — Orbicella annularis — Symbiodiniaceae. Centre the primary framework-building Orbicella colonies along the wall crest
  • Blue tang grazing highway — Acanthurus coeruleus herbivore guild. Locate the carbonate spur patrolled by roving Acanthurus grazing schools
  • Brain coral goby car-wash — Elacatinus evelynae cleaning station. Scan the grooved Pseudodiploria head hosting sharknose goby cleaners

Source: Rohwer et al. (2002), Coral Reefs & Mumby (2006), Science — Multipartite coral holobionts and Caribbean herbivore phase-shift resilience.

Ask together: What happens to tiny flower seedlings in a garden if nobody ever trims tall fast-growing weeds—and how is a coral reef the same?

Field quiz

Kids' question: How do schools of bright blue tang fish help baby corals grow along the reef wall?

  • They mow down fast-growing seaweed so baby corals get room and sunlight
  • They stack heavy rocks with their tails
  • They blow warm air bubbles from the surface
Answer

They mow down fast-growing seaweed so baby corals get room and sunlight

Adults' question: What ecological cascade follows intense grazing by Caribbean parrotfish and surgeonfish guilds (Mumby, 2006)?

  • Suppression of macroalgal turf doubles coral larval recruitment onto bare coralline limestone
  • Complete elimination of Symbiodiniaceae from polyp tissues
  • Acidification of lagoonal aragonite saturation states
  • Conversion of stony reefs into deep aphotic sponge gardens
Answer

Suppression of macroalgal turf doubles coral larval recruitment onto bare coralline limestone
