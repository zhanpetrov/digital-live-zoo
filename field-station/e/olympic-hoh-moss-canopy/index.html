About this place

Investigate Hoh temperate rainforest Sitka spruce nurse-log regeneration, epiphytic bryophytes, and Roosevelt elk.

For kids: Tiptoe into an emerald fairy-tale forest where giant spruce trees stand on stilts grown atop mossy nurse logs!

Olympic Hoh Moss Rainforest · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Mossy Sitka spruce nurse log — Picea sitchensis nurse-log colonnade. Centre a decaying nurse log elevating spruce seedlings above floor moss
  • Draped clubmoss & maple canopy — Selaginella oregana & canopy soil roots. Scan bigleaf maple limbs supporting epiphytic bryophytes and canopy roots
  • Roosevelt elk fern clearing — Cervus canadensis roosevelti herbivory. Locate the sword-fern understory corridor maintained by non-migratory elk

Source: Harmon & Franklin (1989), Ecology; Nadkarni (1984), Science — Nurse-log competition escape & adventitious canopy roots in Olympic rainforests.

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why are fallen giant tree trunks in the Hoh Rain Forest called 'nurse logs'?

  • Spongy damp wood feeds and cradles rows of baby seedlings above thick floor moss
  • Bears wear bandages there
  • Logs float downstream to the ocean
Answer

Spongy damp wood feeds and cradles rows of baby seedlings above thick floor moss

Adults' question: How do Hoh bigleaf maples tap nutrients stored inside their own heavy epiphytic moss canopies?

  • Sprouting adventitious canopy roots directly from upper bark into epiphytic organic mats
  • Dropping all branches every winter to crush mosses
  • Pumping salt water upward from Pacific tides
  • Digesting insects with sticky carnivorous leaves
Answer

Sprouting adventitious canopy roots directly from upper bark into epiphytic organic mats
