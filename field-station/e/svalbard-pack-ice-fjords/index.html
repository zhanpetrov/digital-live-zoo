About this place

Survey High-Arctic fjord glaciers at 79°N where sympagic ice algae, ringed seals, and polar photoperiods govern life.

For kids: Explore a land where the sun shines at midnight in summer and tiny ice plants feed seals and whales!

Svalbard High-Arctic Fjords · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Tidewater glacier wall — Subglacial meltwater plume. Centre the tidewater glacier terminus where subglacial plumes surface
  • Floating brash & floe ice — Pusa hispida haul-out ice. Locate the drift-ice floes used by ringed seals for moulting and pupping
  • Arctic seabird talus peaks — Alle alle guano fertilization. Scan the frost-shattered mountain slopes enriched by seabird marine nutrients

Source: Leu et al. (2011), Progress in Oceanography — Sympagic ice-algal bloom timing & High-Arctic copepod match-mismatch.

Ask together: How would your bedtime feel if the sun stayed bright in the sky at midnight for four whole months?
