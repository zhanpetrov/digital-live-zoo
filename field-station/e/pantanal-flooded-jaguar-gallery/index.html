What to look for

  • Caiman & jaguar river sandbar — Panthera onca cranial-bite ambush bank. Centre the point-bar sandbank where dry-season drawdown concentrates aquatic prey
  • Floating hyacinth capybara rafts — Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris periscope cranial line. Locate the floating Pontederia macrophyte mats grazed by social capybara herds
  • Towering jabiru stork gallery crown — Jabiru mycteria floodplain platform nest. Scan the emergent gallery tree crown supporting perennial Jabiru mycteria nests

Source: Junk et al. (1989) Flood Pulse Concept & Eriksson et al. (2022), Ecology — Pantanal social/aquatic Panthera onca trophic dynamics.

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why are a capybara's eyes, ears, and nostrils all lined up right along the top of its head?

  • So it can look, listen, and breathe like a periscope while hiding underwater!
  • To wear giant water-lily hats in the rain
  • So hummingbirds can land on its nose
Answer

So it can look, listen, and breathe like a periscope while hiding underwater!

Adults' question: How do northern Pantanal riverine jaguars deviate from classic solitary-territorial felid ecology (Eriksson et al., 2022, Ecology)?

  • Superabundant aquatic caiman and fish prey relaxes territoriality, permitting tolerant co-foraging and male coalitions
  • Pantanal jaguars hibernate underwater inside mud burrows
  • Adults subsist strictly on fallen palm nuts year-round
  • Jaguars migrate three thousand kilometres to Andean glaciers
Answer

Superabundant aquatic caiman and fish prey relaxes territoriality, permitting tolerant co-foraging and male coalitions
