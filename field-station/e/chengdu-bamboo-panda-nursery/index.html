About this place

Investigate extreme eutherian neonatal altriciality in giant pandas and seasonal altitudinal tracking of nitrogen-rich shoots.

For kids: Discover how tiny butter-stick-sized panda cubs grow into playful tree climbers chasing tender spring bamboo shoots!

Chengdu Bamboo Panda Nursery · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Spring bamboo-shoot bed — High-protein culm meristems. Locate emerging spring bamboo shoots rich in protein and phosphorus
  • Cub-climbing nursery boughs — Arboreal predator-refuge branches. Centre the stout lateral limbs used by cubs during maternal foraging bouts
  • Sheltered den & mossy hollow — Delayed implantation & natal den. Scan the sheltered basal hollow replicating natal cave and fir-trunk dens

Source: Li & Smith (2020), Journal of Anatomy — Premature ossification arrest in giant panda neonates & Nie et al. (2015), Functional Ecology — Calcium/nitrogen phenology.

Ask together: Imagine holding a baby bear as light as a stick of butter—why do panda moms cradle newborns off the cool ground on their furry paws for weeks?

Field quiz

Kids' question: About how small is a brand-new baby giant panda when it is first born?

  • About as light as a stick of butter
  • As big as a golden retriever dog
  • Bigger than a bicycle
Answer

About as light as a stick of butter

Adults' question: Why is a newborn giant panda roughly 1/900th the mass of its mother—the smallest ratio among eutherian mammals?

  • Truncated post-implantation gestation (~45 days) shifting skeletal ossification to lactation
  • Lack of a chorioallantoic placenta in all ursid carnivores
  • Obligate twin broods splitting maternal blood glucose
  • High-altitude hypoxia halting fetal lung development
Answer

Truncated post-implantation gestation (~45 days) shifting skeletal ossification to lactation
