About this place

Analyze Pithecophaga jefferyi low-aspect-ratio canopy aerodynamics, gliding mammal predation, and prolonged biparental investment.

For kids: Look high into giant rainforest trees where crowned eagles steer like forest jets and raise one precious chick for two years!

Mindanao Canopy Philippine Eagles · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Emergent lauan nest crown — Shorea canopy platform eyrie. Centre the emergent dipterocarp crown supporting epiphytic fern eyries
  • Liana & colugo glide corridor — Cynocephalus volans foraging stratum. Locate the mid-canopy gap traversed by nocturnal Philippine colugos
  • Buttress-root cloud-forest floor — Shallow tropical soil anchorage. Scan the flared buttress roots stabilizing canopy giants in thin volcanic soil

Source: Miranda et al. (2011), Forktail & Salvador & Ibañez (2006) — Pithecophaga jefferyi nesting biology, canopy morphology, and colugo diet.

Ask together: Why would an eagle flying inside a thick forest want shorter, rounder wings instead of the super-long glider wings of an ocean bird?

Field quiz

Kids' question: How do wide rounded wings and a long tail help the Philippine eagle inside thick jungle trees?

  • They let it brake fast and steer sharply around trunks and vines
  • They work as paddles for swimming underwater
  • They glow in the dark like fireflies
Answer

They let it brake fast and steer sharply around trunks and vines

Adults' question: Why does the Philippine eagle exhibit one of the slowest reproductive rates of any bird on Earth?

  • Single-egg clutches followed by ~17 months of post-fledging parental care until hunting mastery
  • Obligate brood parasitism inside hornbill tree cavities
  • Seven-year marine pelagic immaturity across the Pacific
  • Incubation solely via volcanic geothermal soil mounds
Answer

Single-egg clutches followed by ~17 months of post-fledging parental care until hunting mastery
