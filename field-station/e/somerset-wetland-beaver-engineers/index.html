Somerset Wetland Beaver Engineers · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Braided willow leaky dam — Storm-hydrograph attenuating weir. Locate the permeable woody dam crest dispersing peak storm discharge laterally
  • Chisel-gnawed willow coppice — Iron-hardened enamel & willow resprouting. Centre the coppiced Salix bank showing differential incisor wear and multi-stem regrowth
  • Moated dome lodge & underwater door — Castor fiber thermal-moat architecture. Scan the mud-plastered woody lodge whose pond depth submerges entrance tunnels

Source: Puttock et al. (2017), Ecohydrology & Gordon et al. (2015), Science — Beaver dam storm-flow attenuation, water storage, and iron-doped ferrihydrite enamel nanowires.

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why are a Eurasian beaver's front chisel teeth bright orange instead of pearly white?

  • Real iron locked inside the front enamel makes them super-hard and rust-orange
  • Beavers brush their teeth with carrot juice every night
  • Eating dandelion flowers stains them yellow-orange
Answer

Real iron locked inside the front enamel makes them super-hard and rust-orange

Adults' question: What watershed-scale hydrological effect did Puttock et al. (2017) document downstream of reintroduced English beaver dam sequences?

  • Significant attenuation of storm peak discharge, lengthened lag times, elevated baseflow in drought, and sediment/nutrient retention
  • Instantaneous catastrophic draining of upstream aquifers
  • Complete salinization of freshwater headwaters into marine lagoons
  • Elimination of all aquatic invertebrates and salmonid fishes
Answer

Significant attenuation of storm peak discharge, lengthened lag times, elevated baseflow in drought, and sediment/nutrient retention
