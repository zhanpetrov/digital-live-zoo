What to look for

  • Submerged tree-trunk slalom maze — Inia geoffrensis unfused cervical spine. Centre the submerged root-and-trunk labyrinth navigated by Inia geoffrensis
  • Mirror-dark blackwater sonar channel — Collimated melon biosonar beam. Locate the tannin-stained channel where narrow-beam clicks prevent echo clutter
  • Fish-dispersed fruiting canopy — Colossoma macropomum ichthyochory. Scan the floodplain tree crowns evolved for seed dispersal by frugivorous tambaqui

Source: Ladegaard et al. (2015), J. Exp. Biol. & Correa et al. (2015), Ecology — Inia geoffrensis clutter-free biosonar and Amazonian fish seed dispersal.

Field quiz

Kids' question: What special neck adaptation lets an Amazon pink river dolphin weave easily between flooded rainforest trees?

  • Unfused neck bones that let it turn its head sideways left and right!
  • A stretchy giraffe neck hidden inside its tail
  • Tiny wheels under its chin
Answer

Unfused neck bones that let it turn its head sideways left and right!

Adults' question: Why do Amazonian floodplain trees time fruit ripening to peak high-water months rather than the dry season (Correa et al., 2015)?

  • To enlist large frugivorous fishes like tambaqui for long-distance upstream seed dispersal across flooded forests
  • Because insects and birds vanish completely during summer rains
  • To freeze seeds inside cold Andean snowmelt ice
  • To poison herbivorous river turtles with dry bark
Answer

To enlist large frugivorous fishes like tambaqui for long-distance upstream seed dispersal across flooded forests
