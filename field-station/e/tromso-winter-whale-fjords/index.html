About this place

Investigate Orcinus orca cooperative carousel-feeding acoustics and humpback commensalism in overwintering herring fjords.

For kids: Watch clever orcas herd silver fish into giant spinning bait-balls under pink Arctic twilight!

Tromsø Herring-Whale Fjords · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Deep ice-free herring fjord — Clupea harengus overwintering basin. Centre the ice-free Norwegian Atlantic Current fjord trough
  • Glaciated Lyngen-style peaks — Acoustic fjord amphitheatre. Scan the steep metamorphic fjord walls shelving into 300 m water
  • Pastel 'Mørketid' polar twilight — Civil twilight atmospheric scattering. Observe the low-angle solar refraction band along the southern horizon

Source: Similä & Ugarte (1993), Canadian Journal of Zoology — Carousel feeding & underwater tail-slaps by Norwegian killer whales.
