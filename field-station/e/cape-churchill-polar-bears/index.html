About this place

Track Ursus maritimus fasting physiology, solar-trapping pelage, and autumn sea-ice freeze-up on western Hudson Bay.

For kids: Meet giant polar bears with hollow greenhouse fur and warm black skin waiting for winter sea ice!

Cape Churchill Polar Bears · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Leeward snow-pit ridge — Ursus maritimus day bed. Locate the leeward coastal snow ridge used for wind-sheltered resting pits
  • Slush & pancake-ice shore — Autumn grease-ice freeze-up. Centre the shoreline freeze-up zone where grease ice consolidates
  • Subarctic scent-wind sky — Long-range olfactory plume. Scan the onshore wind corridor carrying marine scent plumes

Source: Stirling & Derocher (2012), Global Change Biology — Sea-ice freeze-up phenology and Ursus maritimus energy budgets.

Ask together: If you walked in freezing wind, would you pick hollow greenhouse fur like a polar bear or thick blubber like a seal—and why?

Kids' question: What color is a polar bear's skin hidden underneath its snowy-looking coat?

  • Jet black to soak up warm sunshine
  • Bright pink like a flamingo
  • Icy blue like glacier water
Answer

Jet black to soak up warm sunshine

Adults' question: How do Hudson Bay polar bears sustain muscle mass during months ashore without seal prey?

  • Oxidizing stored seal blubber while recycling urea nitrogen
  • Pursuit-hunting tundra caribou herds daily
  • Entering deep sub-zero permafrost hibernation
  • Grazing protein-rich marine kelp exclusively
Answer

Oxidizing stored seal blubber while recycling urea nitrogen
