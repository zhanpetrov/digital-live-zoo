What to look for

  • Iron-red laterite dust bath — Ferric-oxide laterite & micro-channel skin. Centre the iron-oxide laterite wallow used for UV and ectoparasite shielding
  • Crystal lava-filtered palm pool — Vesicular basalt filtration & Garra mutualism. Locate the spring-fed oasis where lava-filtered water supports hippo-fish mutualisms
  • Green Chyulu volcanic hills — Holocene scoria-cone mist catchment. Scan the young Chyulu volcanic range acting as Tsavo West's mist-and-rain sponge

Source: Martins et al. (2018), Nature Communications — Keratinous epidermal micro-cracking & evaporative cooling in Loxodonta africana.

  • They eat baskets of red tomatoes every morning
  • Their baby fuzz grows in bright crimson

Adults' question: How does the microscopic fracture network in African elephant skin (Martins et al., 2018) solve thermoregulation without sweat glands?

  • Capillary micro-channels spread and hold water/mud up to ten times longer for sustained evaporative cooling
  • Subcutaneous blubber blocks all heat exchange across the torso
  • Open skin pores secrete liquid iron oxide directly from blood hemoglobin
  • Wrinkles reflect 100% of infrared radiation like polished silver
Answer
