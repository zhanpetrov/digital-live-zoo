What to look for

  • Clean spawning-redd gravel — Oxygenated hyporheic redd. Locate rounded gravel substrate ventilated by cold hyporheic flow
  • Current-shadow resting pocket — Kármán street energy conservation. Centre the slackwater eddy where migrating fish utilize Kármán vortices
  • Refractive Snell's window above — Air-water critical angle cone. Scan the bright overhead refraction window watched by holding salmon
