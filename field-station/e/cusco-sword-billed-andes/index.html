About this place

Examine reciprocal coevolution between Ensifera ensifera and long-tubed Passiflora mixta alongside high-altitude Andean hemoglobin physiology.

For kids: Meet the only bird on Earth whose beak is longer than its whole body—drinking from giant hanging trumpet flowers high in the Andes!

Cusco Sword-Billed Hummingbird Peaks · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Hanging Andean passionflower vines — Passiflora mixta — Ensifera lock-and-key. Centre the Passiflora liana edge coevolved exclusively with Ensifera ensifera
  • Paper-bark Polylepis preening perch — Polylepis woodland & pedal preening. Locate the high-elevation Polylepis boughs where Ensifera rests bill-upward
  • Thin-air Cusco mountain sky — Patagona gigas & high-O2 hemoglobin. Scan the high-altitude ridge shared with giant hummingbirds (Patagona gigas)

Source: Abrahamczyk, Souto-Vilarós & Renner (2014), Proc. R. Soc. B & Projecto-Garcia et al. (2013), PNAS — Ensifera–Passiflora coevolution & Andean hemoglobin.

Ask together: Try touching your nose if your nose were longer than your arm—how do you think the sword-billed hummingbird combs its own feathers?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Since a sword-billed hummingbird's beak is longer than its whole body, how does it comb and tidy its feathers?

  • It balances on one leg and scratches its feathers neat with its tiny feet!
  • Other birds line up to brush it with twigs
  • It dips its whole beak into tree sap
Answer

It balances on one leg and scratches its feathers neat with its tiny feet!

Adults' question: What evolutionary pattern did phylogenies reveal when some Passiflora species secondarily shortened their floral tubes (Abrahamczyk et al., 2014)?

  • Extreme specialization rendered Ensifera vulnerable whenever mutualist Passiflora partners declined or switched to bat pollination
  • Ensifera shortened its bill by half within two generations
  • Sword-billed hummingbirds switched entirely to catching alpine trout
  • Short-tubed flowers fused petals shut against all insects
Answer

Extreme specialization rendered Ensifera vulnerable whenever mutualist Passiflora partners declined or switched to bat pollination
