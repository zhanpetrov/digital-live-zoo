What to look for

  • Fluffy subnivium snow blanket — Lemmus lemmus winter runways. Locate deep snowpack trapping geothermal heat inside the subnivium
  • Mountain birch treeline — Betula pubescens ssp. czerepanovii. Centre the subarctic mountain birch ecotone browsing zone
  • Abisko blue-hole clear sky — Orographic rain-shadow microclimate. Scan the rain-shadow sky dome created by the Scandinavian Mountains

Source: Pauli et al. (2013), Frontiers in Ecology and the Environment — The subnivium: aseasonal winter thermal refuge.
