About this place

Examine Ailuropoda melanoleuca radial sesamoid biomechanics, hypothyroid energy economy, and dual-habitat disruptive coloration.

For kids: Meet gentle giant pandas using a built-in sixth 'thumb' and dual snow-and-shadow camouflage in misty bamboo mountains!

Wolong Misty Bamboo Pandas · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Montane arrow-bamboo thicket — Bashania faberi foraging stand. Locate the dense montane bamboo culms gripped via the radial sesamoid
  • Dark fir trunks & mossy boulders — Melanic disruptive shadow matching. Centre the dark subalpine conifer trunks matched by melanic shoulder patches
  • Cloud-wrapped Qionglai ridge — Hypometabolic thermal microclimate. Scan the high cloud-forest canopy buffering summer heat stress

Source: Caro et al. (2017), Behavioral Ecology — Giant panda black-and-white pelage as dual snow/shade camouflage & Nie et al. (2015), Science — Low thyroid metabolism.

Ask together: Try picking up a pencil without bending your thumb—how does a panda's extra wrist bump make holding round bamboo stalks so much easier?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why do giant pandas wear both snowy white AND pitch-black fur at the same time?

  • White hides them in mountain snow and black hides them in dark forest shadows
  • To look like giant soccer balls for eagle friends
  • Because bamboo juice paints stripes on their paws
Answer

White hides them in mountain snow and black hides them in dark forest shadows

Adults' question: Which physiological adaptation allows giant pandas to survive on nutrient-poor bamboo with a carnivore-like short gut?

  • Ultra-low thyroid hormone output (DUOX2) cutting basal metabolism to ~38% of eutherian norms
  • A four-chambered ruminant foregut fermenting cellulose for 72 hours
  • Six-month deep hibernation beneath alpine permafrost
  • Photosynthetic symbiotic algae inside translucent guard hairs
Answer

Ultra-low thyroid hormone output (DUOX2) cutting basal metabolism to ~38% of eutherian norms
