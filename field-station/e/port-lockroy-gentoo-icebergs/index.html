About this place

Study Pygoscelis papua 36 km/h hydrodynamic wing-propulsion, pebble-nest microtopography, and snow-highway energetics.

For kids: Meet the fastest underwater swimming birds on Earth, who propose with shiny smooth pebbles!

Port Lockroy Gentoo Cove · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Snow-free pebble nest knoll — Pygoscelis papua thermal drainage nest. Locate the exposed dark bedrock knoll where pebble rings elevate eggs above meltwater
  • Packed penguin snow highway — Communal snow-trench energetics. Centre the snow-slope corridor trampled into firm commuting trails
  • Brash-ice krill harbour — 36 km/h underwater flight zone. Survey the sheltered channel where gentoos execute micro-bubble drag-reduction bursts

Source: Bannasch (1995) & Lynch et al. (2012), Ecology — Hydrofoil flipper drag reduction & Pygoscelis range expansion on the Antarctic Peninsula.

Ask together: Why would a pile of rocks make a much safer nest in melting summer snow than soft grass or feathers?

Adults' question: How does feather-trapped air assist diving penguins both thermally and hydrodynamically?

  • Insulates skin during shallow dives and vents micro-bubbles to slash boundary-layer drag on ascent
  • Inflates hollow wing bones for floating
  • Provides oxygen directly into gill slits
  • Produces ultrasonic krill-stunning pops
