What to look for

  • Garúa mist mud wallow — Chelonoidis porteri thermal pool. Centre the garúa-fed highland wallow used for nocturnal heat retention
  • Finch tick-cleaning turf — Geospiza fuliginosa cleaning station. Locate the grazed meadow turf where ground finches solicit cleaning postures
  • Giant daisy-tree mist canopy — Scalesia pedunculata fog interception. Scan the endemic Scalesia canopy harvesting horizontal ocean fog

Source: Chiari et al. (2017), Scientific Reports & MacFarland & Reeder (1974), Science — Saddleback vs domed Chelonoidis mechanics and finch mutualism.
