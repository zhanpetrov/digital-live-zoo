About this place

Track Hippopotamus amphibius biogenic silicon transport into river food webs and red hipposudoric acid photoprotection.

For kids: Glide past Zambezi reed channels where chubby hippos make their own rosy sunscreen and feed river fish!

Zambezi Hippos & Carmine Cliffs · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Deep slackwater hippo lagoon — Hipposudoric acid & negative buoyancy. Centre the slackwater channel pod exhibiting periscopic cranial alignment
  • Floodplain grazing lawn & canal — Biogenic silica (BSi) riverine pump. Locate the alluvial grazing lawn supplying biogenic silicon to the Zambezi food web
  • Sandy riverbank bee-eater cliff — Merops nubicoides colonial nesting bank. Scan the vertical alluvial bank excavated by colonial southern carmine bee-eaters

Source: Schoelynck et al. (2019), Science Advances & Saikawa et al. (2004), Nature — Hippo-vectored river silicon pump & hipposudoric acid sunscreen chemistry.
