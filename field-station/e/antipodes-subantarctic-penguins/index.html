About this place

Investigate Eudyptes sclateri obligate brood reduction, egg-size dimorphism, and Furious Fifties foraging ecology.

For kids: Meet spiky-browed golden-crested penguins hopping onto wild volcanic boulders in the Southern Ocean!

Antipodes Crested Penguins · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Volcanic surf-landing boulders — Eudyptes sclateri colony platform. Centre the wave-swept volcanic boulder terrace hosting penguin nest scrapes
  • Wind-sculpted tussock slopes — Poa litorosa subantarctic grassland. Locate the peat-forming tussock slopes supporting endemic Antipodes parakeets
  • Furious Fifties Southern Ocean swell — Antarctic Circumpolar Current. Scan the unobstructed Southern Ocean fetch driven by the Circumpolar Current

Source: Davis et al. (2022), PLOS ONE — Resolving the 60-year mystery of first-egg loss and egg-size dimorphism in Eudyptes sclateri.
