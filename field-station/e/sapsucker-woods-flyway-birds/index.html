What to look for

  • Chickadee bark-pantry birches — Poecile atricapillus hippocampal cache map. Centre the birch-hemlock trunks used for scatter-hoarding thousands of seeds
  • Sapsucker maple soda-fountain — Sphyrapicus varius keystone sap wells. Locate hardwood trunks drilled with grid-like phloem sap wells
  • Atlantic Flyway quantum night sky — Cryptochrome-4 radical-pair compass. Scan the Atlantic Flyway airspace navigated via retinal radical-pair physics

Source: Xu et al. (2021), Nature; Barnea & Nottebohm (1994), PNAS — Retinal cryptochrome-4 magnetoreception & seasonal hippocampal neurogenesis.
