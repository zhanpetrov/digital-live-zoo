About this place

Examine Adansonia grandidieri parenchymatous water storage, sphingid/lemur nocturnal pollination, and island endemism.

For kids: Stand beneath towering upside-down-looking baobab trees that store rainwater like barrels and bloom at dusk for lemurs!

Madagascar Giant Baobab Ark · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Cylindrical water-barrel trunk — Adansonia grandidieri parenchyma capacitance. Centre the massive pachycaul trunk storing monsoon water in low-density parenchyma
  • Upside-down root-like crown — Sphingophilic & lemur nocturnal blossoms. Scan the flat-topped crown bearing nocturnal blossoms pollinated by hawk-moths and lemurs
  • Seasonal mirror pool & red track — Gondwanan island ark endemism (~90%). Locate the seasonal Menabe wetland remnant beside the historic dry-forest corridor

Source: Baum (1995), Annals of the Missouri Botanical Garden & Chapotin et al. (2006), Plant, Cell & Environment — Baobab pollination syndromes & stem water capacitance.

Ask together: Why do evening-blooming flowers smell extra sweet and shine pale white instead of bright daytime red?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Out of all eight species of baobab trees on Planet Earth, how many are native ONLY to the island of Madagascar?

  • Six out of eight live nowhere else in the wild!
  • Zero—baobabs only grow in snowy Antarctica
  • Only tiny bonsai baobabs in flowerpots
Answer

Six out of eight live nowhere else in the wild!

Adults' question: What primary physiological role does stored water inside Adansonia grandidieri parenchymatous wood serve (Chapotin et al., 2006)?

  • Maintaining mechanical stem turgor and enabling pre-rain leaf flushing before monsoon onset
  • Circulating boiling geothermal steam to melt winter frost
  • Excreting saltwater brine onto surrounding competitors
  • Digesting trapped insects inside hollow pitcher branches
Answer

Maintaining mechanical stem turgor and enabling pre-rain leaf flushing before monsoon onset
