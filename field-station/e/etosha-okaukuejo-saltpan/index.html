About this place

Etosha Starlight Rhino Spring · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Limestone artesian waterhole — Calcrete contact spring discharge. Centre the calcrete contact spring concentrating Etosha's diel waterhole schedule
  • Great white Etosha clay pan — Endorheic evaporite clay basin. Scan the hyper-saline endorheic clay depression stretching across northern Namibia
  • Arid thornbush & oryx trail — Oryx gazella carotid rete thermoregulation. Locate the dwarf shrubland browsed by heat-adapted Oryx gazella

Source: Gaynor et al. (2018), Science & Valeix et al. (2007), Behavioral Ecology — Temporal niche partitioning & diel waterhole scheduling in African megafauna.

Kids' question: How can you easily tell a browsing black rhino apart from a grazing white rhino?

  • By its pointed, finger-like upper lip built for plucking bushes
  • By glow-in-the-dark purple stripes
  • By giant feathery wings
Answer

By its pointed, finger-like upper lip built for plucking bushes

Adults' question: How does temporal niche partitioning around Etosha's springs reduce physiological stress and interspecific conflict?

  • Staggered day/night arrival schedules cut waterhole crowding and reduce nocturnal evaporative heat loss
  • All herbivores drink exclusively at solar noon to avoid starlight
  • Salt crusts freeze solid every evening at the equator
  • Spring water flows only for ten minutes at midnight
Answer

Staggered day/night arrival schedules cut waterhole crowding and reduce nocturnal evaporative heat loss

By day
Springbok, plains zebras, gemsbok (oryx), kudu & Angolan giraffes Jugular one-way valves and elastic cranial blood-vessel walls prevent dangerous blood-pressure spikes when giraffes lower their heads 2 m below the heart.
By night
Endangered black rhinos, desert-adapted elephants, lions & brown hyenas Okaukuejo is one of Earth's premier nocturnal observation sites for free-ranging Diceros bicornis social greetings at permanent water.
