Congo Basin Peaceful Bonobos · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Swinging liana & fruit highway — Arboreal suspensory brachiation corridor. Scan the liana-linked canopy used for agile suspensory travel and fruit sharing
  • Forest stream of the Congo basin — Congo River allopatric speciation barrier. Centre the riverine margin illustrating the Congo River biogeographic barrier
  • Abundant forest-floor herb glade — Terrestrial herbaceous vegetation (THV). Locate the dense terrestrial herbaceous patch enabling stable female associations

Source: Tan & Hare (2013), PLOS ONE & Prüfer et al. (2012), Nature — Xenophilic prosociality, female coalitions & genomic divergence in Pan paniscus.
