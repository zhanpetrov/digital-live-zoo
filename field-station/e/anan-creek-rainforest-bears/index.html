About this place

Observe rare Ursus americanus and Ursus arctos sympatry driven by hyper-abundant pink salmon in Tongass old growth.

For kids: Visit a mossy waterfall canyon where black bears and brown bears share the same giant salmon buffet!

Anan Creek Bear Gorge · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Bedrock salmon plunge pool — Oncorhynchus gorbuscha bottleneck. Centre the bedrock plunge pool concentrating migrating pink salmon
  • Old-growth refuge spruces — Arboreal cub-refuge trees. Scan the thick-barked Sitka spruce & western hemlock refuge trees
  • Mist-drenched fern & moss wall — Temperate rainforest bryophyte mat. Locate the bryophyte-draped canyon wall storing rainforest moisture

Source: Chi & Gilbert (1999), Ursus — Temporal & microhabitat partitioning between sympatric black and brown bears at Anan Creek.

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why are black bears much better tree-climbers than big coastal brown bears?

  • Shorter, sharply curved claws that hook right into tree bark
  • Wings hidden under their arms
  • Lighter hollow bird bones
Answer

Shorter, sharply curved claws that hook right into tree bark

Adults' question: How do black bears at Anan Creek minimize direct conflict with dominant coastal brown bears?

  • Fishing mostly in bright midday hours while brown bears peak at dawn, dusk, and night, plus using steep escape trees
  • Digging underwater tunnels beneath the waterfall
  • Forming coordinated packs of ten black bears
  • Mimicking bald eagle calls
Answer

Fishing mostly in bright midday hours while brown bears peak at dawn, dusk, and night, plus using steep escape trees
