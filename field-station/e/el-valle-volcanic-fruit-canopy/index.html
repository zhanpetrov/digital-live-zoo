About this place

Examine Pleistocene caldera soil fertility, avian frugivore gape-width guilds, and Janzen-Connell seed-dispersal mutualisms.

For kids: Step inside a giant sleeping volcano crater filled with cool misty forest where rainbow tanagers and aracaris plant baby trees!

El Valle Volcanic Crater Fruit Garden · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • La India Dormida volcanic crater rim — Pleistocene dacitic caldera microclimate. Scan the collapsed Pleistocene caldera ridge intercepting Caribbean trade-wind clouds
  • Rainbow tanager & aracari fruit bough — Gape-limited vs large-seed frugivore guilds. Centre the sub-canopy fruiting branches shared by Ramphocelus tanagers and Pteroglossus aracaris
  • Volcanic creek & seedling gap — Janzen-Connell pathogen-escape zone. Locate the sunny streamside canopy gap where dispersed seeds escape host-specific pathogens

Source: Howe & Smallwood (1982), Annu. Rev. Ecol. Syst. & Holbrook & Loiselle (2009), Biotropica — Neotropical frugivory & toucan seed dispersal.

Ask together: Why does a baby rainforest tree grow much better when a bird carries its seed far away instead of dropping right in its parent tree's shadow?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Why do rainforest trees wrap their seeds inside sweet, brightly colored fruit pulp?

  • To reward hungry birds for carrying clean seeds to sunny new places to sprout!
  • To keep seeds floating high in the clouds
  • To scare away friendly butterflies
Answer

To reward hungry birds for carrying clean seeds to sunny new places to sprout!

Adults' question: Why can't small-gaped songbirds replace collared aracaris and toucans in dispersing climax canopy nutmeg trees like Virola (Holbrook & Loiselle, 2009)?

  • Small birds peck pulp in place and drop seeds beneath the parent crown, whereas wide-gaped toucans swallow large seeds whole and regurgitate them intact hundreds of metres away
  • Tanagers digest granite stones inside muscular crops
  • Nutmeg seeds germinate exclusively inside salt-water mangroves
  • Aracaris bury seeds three metres underground like moles
Answer

Small birds peck pulp in place and drop seeds beneath the parent crown, whereas wide-gaped toucans swallow large seeds whole and regurgitate them intact hundreds of metres away
