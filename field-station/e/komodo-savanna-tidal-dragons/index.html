About this place

Examine Varanus komodoensis stereo vomeronasal chemotaxis, chain-mail osteoderms, and iron-enriched serrated enamel.

For kids: Explore dry palm-dotted islands where Earth's biggest lizards smell the breeze in stereo with forked tongues and grow orange iron-coated teeth!

What to look for

  • Monsoon-shadow lontar savanna ridge — Stereo vomeronasal scent plume. Locate the dry lontar-palm ridge funneling trade-wind odor plumes
  • Sun-warmed volcanic trail & burrows — Osteoderm armor & iron-capped ziphodonty. Centre the sunlit lowland flat where adult monitors thermoregulate
  • Tamarind canopy & tidal beach — Ontogenetic arboreal niche shift. Scan the coastal tamarind crowns sheltering arboreal juvenile monitors

Source: LeBlanc et al. (2024), Nature Ecology & Evolution — Iron-coated serrated tooth enamel in Varanus komodoensis; Auffenberg (1981) — Stereo vomeronasal tracking.
