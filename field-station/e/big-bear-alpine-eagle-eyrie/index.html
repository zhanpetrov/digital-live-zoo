About this place

Examine high-altitude Haliaeetus leucocephalus brood-patch thermoregulation and subzero spring incubation.

For kids: Perch high in snowy mountain pine treetops where devoted eagle parents keep eggs toasty under spring blizzards!

Big Bear Alpine Eagle Eyrie · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Jeffrey pine treetop eyrie — Pinus jeffreyi vascular brood-patch bowl. Centre the emergent Jeffrey pine crown supporting the insulated stick bowl
  • Alpine trout & coot lake — Oncorhynchus mykiss & Fulica americana prey. Locate the ice-free open-water patches supplying trout and waterfowl
  • San Bernardino snow-storm crest — Orographic Pacific winter storm track. Scan the two-thousand-metre granite ridgeline intercepting Pacific winter lows

Source: Bortolotti & Wiebe (1993), Wilson Bulletin; Webb (1987), Condor — Avian brood-patch vascularity & subzero raptor incubation.

Field quiz

Kids' question: How does an eagle parent pass cozy warmth straight from its body into its eggs during snowstorms?

  • A special bare, warm skin spot on its chest called a brood patch
  • A tiny electric blanket made of pinecones
  • Breathing hot fire like a dragon
Answer

A special bare, warm skin spot on its chest called a brood patch

Adults' question: What physiological change creates a bald eagle's brood patch prior to late-winter egg laying?

  • Prolactin-mediated ventral defeathering paired with dermal edema and dense capillary vascularization
  • Calcification of breastbone feathers into heat mirrors
  • Shivering wingtips dipped into thermal springs
  • Fermenting pine resin inside the nest rim
Answer

Prolactin-mediated ventral defeathering paired with dermal edema and dense capillary vascularization
