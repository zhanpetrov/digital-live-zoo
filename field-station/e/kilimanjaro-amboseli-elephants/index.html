Amboseli & Kilimanjaro Springs · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Kilimanjaro volcanic snow dome — Subterranean basaltic recharge zone. Scan the Kibo volcanic massif supplying Amboseli's subterranean lava aquifers
  • Emerald papyrus & sedge spring — Enkongo Narok perennial hydro-refugium. Centre the perennial spring-fed sedge swamp sustaining dry-season elephant biomass
  • Dusty elephant family trail — Pacinian corpuscle seismic reception. Locate the compacted lakebed substrate conducting low-frequency Rayleigh waves

Source: Payne, Langbauer & Thomas (1986), Behavioral Ecology and Sociobiology; O'Connell-Rodwell (2007) — Infrasonic vocalizations & Rayleigh seismic wave detection in Loxodonta africana.

Adults' question: Which dual physical pathway allows Loxodonta africana to coordinate herd movements over multi-kilometre distances?

  • 14–24 Hz acoustic infrasound paired with ground-coupled Rayleigh waves sensed by foot mechanoreceptors
  • Ultrasonic 80 kHz echolocation clicks bounced off cloud layers
