What to look for

  • Wind-scoured sedge clearing — Permafrost active-layer forage. Locate the wind-thinned snow pasture where herds crater for cured sedges
  • Drunken-forest permafrost spruces — Picea mariana permafrost root mat. Centre the black spruce treeline rooted above shallow permafrost
  • Phalanx lookout knoll — Cooperative anti-predator ring. Scan the elevated vantage point suited for 360° wolf detection
