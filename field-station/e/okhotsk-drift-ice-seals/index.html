Okhotsk Drift-Ice Seal Floes

Mombetsu & Shiretoko, Hokkaido, Japan · Arctic & Subarctic · polar

Start the 360° expedition →

About this place

Examine Amur River freshwater stratification driving 44°N sea ice and Phoca largha lanugo pup thermoregulation.

For kids: Visit the southernmost winter sea ice in the northern half of Earth, where fluffy white seal pups ride ice rafts!

Okhotsk Drift-Ice Seal Floes · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Ryuhyo drift-ice floes — Amur halocline seasonal pack ice. Centre the seasonal drift-ice floes advected southward by the East Sakhalin Current
  • Spotted seal nursery zone — Phoca largha lanugo pupping ice. Locate the pack-ice margin used by spotted seal triads
  • Steller's sea eagle coastal perch — Haliaeetus pelagicus wintering coast. Scan the coastal headlands used by wintering Steller's sea eagles

Source: Ohshima et al. (2013), Nature Geoscience & Trukhin (2009) — Amur halocline sea-ice conveyor & Phoca largha breeding ecology.

Ask together: Hokkaido is at the same latitude as sunny southern France—why does ocean ice float all the way down here?

Kids' question: Why do spotted seal pups grow up so fast on winter drift ice?

  • Mom's super-creamy milk is half rich fat!
  • They eat giant snowballs
  • They sunbathe in tropical heat
Answer

Mom's super-creamy milk is half rich fat!

Adults' question: Which oceanographic feature enables sea ice to form in the Sea of Okhotsk at 44°N?

  • Low-salinity surface stratification from Amur River inflow plus Siberian katabatic winds
  • Submarine permafrost vents freezing the seabed
  • Antarctic icebergs drifting across the equator
  • Absence of ocean tides
Answer

Low-salinity surface stratification from Amur River inflow plus Siberian katabatic winds
