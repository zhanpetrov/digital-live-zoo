What to look for

  • Accordion water-tank ribs & boot hole — Pleated parenchyma & callus 'saguaro boot'. Centre the fluted saguaro stem and healed Gila woodpecker nest cavity
  • Night-blooming nectar crown — Leptonycteris yerbabuenae chiropterophily. Scan the upper arm tips where nocturnal flowers recruit lesser long-nosed bats
  • Green-bark palo verde nurse tree — Parkinsonia microphylla seedling nurse. Locate the photosynthetic-barked palo verde nurse tree shading saguaro recruits

Source: Fleming et al. (2001), Ecological Monographs; Nobel (1988) — Carnegiea gigantea biophysics, CAM water storage & bat mutualism.

Kids' question: How do the pleats on a giant saguaro cactus work right after a summer desert rainstorm?

  • They stretch wide like an accordion to store tonnes of rainwater inside!
  • They play musical desert tunes in the wind
  • They flap like wings to fan lizards
Answer

They stretch wide like an accordion to store tonnes of rainwater inside!

Adults' question: How does Crassulacean Acid Metabolism (CAM) allow Carnegiea gigantea to photosynthesize with minimal desert water loss?

  • Opening stomata exclusively at cool night hours to fix CO2 as malic acid before daytime Calvin-cycle processing
  • Pumping groundwater from three hundred metres deep with taproots
  • Shedding succulent outer skin after every thunderstorm
  • Absorbing carbon exclusively through root hairs
Answer

Opening stomata exclusively at cool night hours to fix CO2 as malic acid before daytime Calvin-cycle processing
