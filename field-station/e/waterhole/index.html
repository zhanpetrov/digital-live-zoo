Quiver Tree Forest 360°

What to look for

  • Quiver tree — Aloidendron dichotomum. Log the largest Aloidendron dichotomum
  • Dolerite boulders — Karoo dolerite, about 180 million years old. Log the dark boulder field
  • Desert grass — Stipagrostis drought-tolerant bunch grass. Log the perennial grass tuft
  • Low sun, long shadows — Late afternoon crepuscular window. Log the sun's position
  • The Quiver Tree Forest — About 250 trees, protected since 1955. Log the stand on the horizon

Source: Foden et al. (2007), Diversity and Distributions — Poleward & upslope range shifts in Namib Aloidendron dichotomum.

Ask together: If a quiver tree stores water inside its spongy trunk like a giant canteen, where would you hide water if you were a desert plant?
