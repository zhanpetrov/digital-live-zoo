What to look for

  • Sheltered volcanic basalt scrape — Bubo bubo cliff-ledge nest scrape. Locate the rain-sheltered basalt cliff recess scraped clean for eagle-owl eggs
  • Whisper-quiet valley glide path — Leading-edge vortex micro-serrations. Centre the cliff-foot hunting corridor where owl wing serrations suppress >2 kHz noise
  • Owl-pellet talus & songpost — Regurgitated osteological pellets. Scan the cliff-top songpost rock where territorial hooting and regurgitated pellets accumulate

Source: Graham (1934), J. Aeronautical Sciences & Jaworski & Peake (2013), Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics — Aeroacoustic mechanisms of silent owl flight.

Ask together: Wave a stiff ruler fast through the air—hear that whoosh? How do soft, fringed edges on an owl's feathers make the whoosh disappear?

Field quiz

Kids' question: Which human inventions borrowed the silent-flight secret of Eurasian eagle-owl feathers?

  • Whisper-quiet bullet trains, wind turbines, and cooling fans
  • Loud fireworks and brass marching drums
  • Submarine anchors and cast-iron frying pans
Answer

Whisper-quiet bullet trains, wind turbines, and cooling fans

Adults' question: Which trio of feather structures eliminates aerodynamic noise above 2 kHz in flying Bubo bubo?

  • Leading-edge serrations, velvety dorsal down pile, and compliant trailing-edge fringes
  • Rigid titanium-hardened primary shafts with razor-straight edges
  • Mucus-coated waterproof scales borrowed from diving penguins
  • Inflatable air bladders along the wingtips
Answer

Leading-edge serrations, velvety dorsal down pile, and compliant trailing-edge fringes

By day
Cryptically plumaged female eagle-owl ('Lotte') & downy owlets sleeping on rock Monitored by Gesellschaft zur Erhaltung der Eulen (EGE), quarries and volcanic crags drove Bubo bubo's dramatic comeback across Germany.
By night
Active eagle-owl parents exchanging prey, ear-tuft displays & deep hooting Plumicorns ('ear tufts') have zero auditory function; eagle-owls erect or flatten them for intraspecific visual signaling and silhouette breaking.
