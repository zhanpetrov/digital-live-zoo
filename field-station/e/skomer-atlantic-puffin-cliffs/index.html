About this place

Investigate Fratercula arctica wing-propelled diving, backward-pointing palatal denticles, and UV-luminescent bill ridges.

For kids: Visit windy Welsh sea cliffs where rainbow-beaked puffins fly underwater and stack shiny fish like neat sandwiches!

Skomer Atlantic Puffin Cliffs · 360° Habitat Camp

What to look for

  • Honeycombed thrift burrow turf — Fratercula arctica puffling burrows. Locate the Armeria maritima turf honeycombed with puffin nest tunnels
  • Celtic Sea sand-eel current — Ammodytes marinus foraging front. Centre the tidal mixing front where alcids pursue schooling sand eels
  • Guillemot & shearwater ledges — Puffinus puffinus nocturnal colony. Scan the sheer basalt ledges shared by guillemots, razorbills, and nocturnal shearwaters

Source: Dunning, Jamie et al. (2018), Ibis & Wanless et al. (2005) — Ultraviolet photoluminescence in Fratercula arctica breeding bills and Ammodytes sand-eel energetics.

Field quiz

Kids' question: What secret trick lets an Atlantic puffin hold a whole row of slippery fish in its beak at once?

  • Tiny backward-pointing spikes on the roof of its mouth and a strong tongue
  • Sticky honey smeared inside its cheeks
  • A stretchy throat pouch like a pelican
Answer

Tiny backward-pointing spikes on the roof of its mouth and a strong tongue

Adults' question: What unexpected optical property did Dunning, Jamie et al. (2018) discover in the breeding bill of Fratercula arctica?

  • Photoluminescence of yellow bill ridges under ultraviolet excitation
  • Infrared thermal emission used to stun schooling fish in dark water
  • Polarized mirror scales shedding saltwater spray
  • Bioluminescent symbiotic bacteria living inside the nostrils
Answer

Photoluminescence of yellow bill ridges under ultraviolet excitation

By night
350,000 Manx shearwaters returning under darkness to avoid gulls Skomer hosts roughly half the world's breeding Manx shearwaters (Puffinus puffinus), which commute ashore strictly on moonless nights.
