Privacy

Updated October 3, 2026

Digital Live Zoo is a free page of live animal cams. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or payment details. This page explains what is collected when you visit, and by whom.

Video from YouTube

Every cam plays in YouTube’s embedded player, straight from the cam owner’s channel. When a player loads, YouTube receives information such as your IP address and browser details, and may set cookies, under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not show cams that their owners mark as made for kids.

Visit statistics

We use PostHog to count visits and see which features are used: which cams go on the Main Stage, which filters are picked, and when a wall is opened. It records an anonymous visitor ID, not who you are. We use this only to improve the zoo.

Advertising

The page may show one ad at the top, served by Google AdSense. No ads appear inside or over the video.

  • Google and other ad companies use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this site and to other sites.
  • Google’s advertising cookies let it and its partners choose ads for you based on those visits. How Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google’s My Ad Center. You can also opt out of personalized ads from many other ad companies at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Stored on your device

Your browser keeps the cams you add to your wall and a list of cams that cannot play outside YouTube, so the page remembers them next time. This stays on your device; clearing site data removes it.

Children

The zoo is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
