About this cam

Bat houses in Osteen, central Florida, run by the nonprofit Fly By Night, with roughly 800 Brazilian free-tailed bats in each. They start leaving just before sunset and swarm back just before sunrise, Eastern time; the stream has run around the clock since 2008.
