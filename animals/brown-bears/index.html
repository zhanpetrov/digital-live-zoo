Live brown bear cams

Live brown bear cams from Brooks Falls and the Brooks River in Katmai National Park and from Anan Creek in Alaska, where bears gather to catch salmon, plus rescued bears in Romania and Russia. In Alaska the action follows the salmon: Brooks Falls is busiest in July and again in September, Anan Creek in July and August. By October the bears are at their heaviest and soon head off to their dens.
