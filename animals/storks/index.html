Live stork nest cams

Live stork nest cams on church roofs, chimneys and poles across Germany and the Czech Republic, and a rare black stork nest deep in the forest of Estonia. White storks fly back from Africa in March and April, often to the same nest every year, lay two to five eggs and raise their chicks through June and July. By late August the young are gone, and most nests stay empty through the winter.
