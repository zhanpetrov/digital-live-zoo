Live hedgehog cams

Live hedgehog cams from gardens and feeding stations in Germany, the Netherlands and England. Hedgehogs come out after dark, so these cams switch to infrared at night, and the best time to watch is from late evening until the small hours. From about November to March most hedgehogs hibernate, and the feeders go quiet until spring.
