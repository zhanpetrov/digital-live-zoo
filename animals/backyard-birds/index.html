Live bird feeder cams

Live bird feeder cams from backyards and nature centres in the United States, Germany, Norway, the Czech Republic and South Africa. Feeders are busiest in the first hours after sunrise and again before dusk, and in winter, when natural food is scarce. Look for cardinals, blue jays and woodpeckers in North America, tits, finches and robins in Europe, and the occasional squirrel, deer or hawk drawn in by the crowd.
