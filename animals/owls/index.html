Live owl cams

Live owl cams from nest boxes, barns, church towers and forests in England, the Netherlands, Germany, Italy, Latvia and the United States. Most owls are active at night, so these cams use infrared light and show a grey picture after dark; dusk is the best time to catch adults leaving to hunt or returning with prey. Barn owls can breed almost any time of year, while eagle-owls and tawny owls nest in late winter and spring.
