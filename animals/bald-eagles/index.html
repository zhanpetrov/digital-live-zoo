Live bald eagle nest cams

Live bald eagle nest cams across the United States: on the California Channel Islands and at Big Bear Lake, in Alaska, Iowa, Michigan, Ohio, Louisiana, Pittsburgh and Florida. Bald eagles reuse and add to the same nest every year, and some nests grow to weigh more than a ton. The season starts early: eggs come as soon as November in Florida and around February further north, and eaglets fledge about three months after hatching.
