Live seal and sea lion cams

Live seal cams from rescue centres in the Netherlands, Namibia and Japan, where injured and orphaned seals recover before release, and from wild beaches in British Columbia and Rhode Island. Harbour and grey seals haul out at low tide to rest and warm up, so wild cams are busiest when the water is low. Underwater cams show how agile seals are once they are back in the water.
