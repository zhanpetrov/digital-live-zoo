Live cat and kitten cams

Live cat cams from rescues, shelters and a cat island: kitten nurseries in Los Angeles, Tucson and Langley, free-roaming shelter cats in Ohio and the Netherlands, rescue rooms in Japan and South Korea. Many of these cats are waiting for adoption, and some rescues stream so that viewers can follow a litter from birth to new home. Mealtimes and play sessions with volunteers are the liveliest moments.
