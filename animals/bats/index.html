Live bat cams

Live bat cams from the United States: cave colonies of free-tailed bats in New Mexico and Texas, a bat house in central Florida, and fruit bats called flying foxes at a Florida sanctuary. Free-tailed bats leave their roosts around dusk, and in summer Bracken Cave in Texas holds the largest bat colony in the world, up to 20 million bats. Brazilian and Mexican free-tailed bats are two names for the same species.
